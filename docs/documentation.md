# CHAPTER 1 – INTRODUCTION

# 1.1 Background of the Project

Barangay offices provide different services that residents need for their daily activities and other personal requirements. These may include barangay clearance, certificate of residency, and other documents or services provided by the barangay. However, the process of requesting these services is often done through face-to-face transactions. Residents may need to visit the barangay office to ask about available services, check the requirements, submit their requests, and follow up on the progress of their documents.

For some residents, this process can be inconvenient and time-consuming. They may need to make several trips to the barangay office just to ask for updates or check if their requested documents are already available. This can be difficult for residents who have work, school, or other responsibilities. There can also be delays when there are many residents requesting services at the same time.

On the side of barangay officials, handling several requests and keeping records of transactions can also be challenging when the process is mostly done manually. Officials need to review requests, check the information provided by residents, update the status of each request, and keep records of completed transactions. Without an organized system, monitoring different requests and finding previous records may take more time.

To help address these concerns, the proposed system is designed to provide a more convenient way for residents of Barangay Cuambugan to access selected barangay services. The system will allow residents to view available services and their requirements, submit requests online, and monitor the progress of their requests. Residents will also be able to receive email notifications regarding important updates about their requests.

The system will also provide a feature for setting the scheduled date for claiming requested documents or services. This can help residents know when they need to visit the barangay office and reduce unnecessary follow-up visits. For barangay officials, the system will provide tools for reviewing and processing requests, updating request statuses, setting claim dates, and maintaining organized records.

In addition, the system can keep records of submitted and completed requests. These records can help officials monitor the number of requests received and identify which documents or services are most frequently requested. This information may also help the barangay better understand the common service needs of its residents.

Overall, the proposed system aims to make the process of requesting selected barangay services more convenient, organized, and accessible. It is intended to reduce unnecessary visits, provide residents with easier access to request information and updates, and help barangay officials manage service requests and records more efficiently. Although some documents may still require physical claiming at the barangay office, the system can provide a more organized way to handle the request process from submission up to completion.


# 1.2 Statement of the Problem

The current process of requesting barangay services often requires residents to visit the barangay office to ask about available services, submit requirements, follow up on their requests, and check when their documents are ready. This can take time and may be inconvenient, especially when residents need to make several visits to the office. For barangay officials, handling and organizing multiple requests and records manually can also make the process more difficult to manage.

To help address these concerns, the proposed system will provide an online platform for selected barangay services in Barangay Cuambugan. It will allow residents to view available services, submit requests, track their request status, and receive email updates. It will also allow authorized barangay staff to manage requests, update their status, set claim schedules, and maintain organized records.

1. How can the system provide residents with an easier way to view available barangay services and their requirements?
2. How can the system allow residents to submit requests online for selected barangay services?
3. How can the system help residents monitor the status and progress of their submitted requests?
4. How can the system provide residents with email notifications about updates to their requests?
5. How can the system help barangay officials review, process, and update residents' service requests?
6. How can the system help officials set the claim date for requested documents or services?
7. How can the system help maintain organized records of submitted and completed requests?
8. How can the system help identify the most frequently requested barangay services or documents?

# 1.4 Scope and Limitations

The proposed system focuses on the development of a web-based barangay service request system for Barangay Cuambugan. It is designed to provide residents with a more convenient and organized way to access selected barangay services through an online platform. Residents will be able to create an account, log in, view available barangay services and their requirements, and submit service requests online. The system will also allow residents to provide the necessary information and documents, monitor the status of their requests, view their previous and completed requests, and receive email notifications regarding important updates. Residents can also view the scheduled date for claiming their requested documents or services.

For authorized barangay officials, the system will provide features for managing and processing submitted requests. Officials will be able to review requests, check the information provided by residents, update request statuses such as Pending, Processing, Approved, and Completed, and set the scheduled date for claiming requested documents or services. The system will also maintain organized records of submitted and completed requests. These records can help officials monitor service requests and identify the documents or services that are frequently requested by residents. The system is intended to support the existing work of barangay officials and provide a more organized way of handling selected service requests.

The system is limited to selected barangay services offered by Barangay Cuambugan and does not cover all possible services provided by the barangay. The system mainly focuses on the submission, monitoring, and management of service requests and does not completely replace the existing responsibilities and decision-making of barangay officials. All submitted requests still need to be reviewed and processed by authorized officials before they can be approved or completed.

Some official documents or services may still require physical claiming at the barangay office even when the request has been submitted and processed through the system. The system also requires a stable internet connection for residents and barangay officials to access its online features. Email notifications depend on the resident providing a valid email address and having access to their email account. The system does not include online payment processing for barangay fees or other possible charges. Lastly, the system is specifically designed for Barangay Cuambugan and is not intended to automatically cover or manage the services of other barangays.

# 1.5 Target Users / Beneficiaries

The completed system is intended for residents and authorized barangay officials or staff of Barangay Cuambugan. Each user group has a different role in using the system and can benefit from the features provided.

Residents are the primary users of the system. They can also receive email notifications and view the scheduled date for claiming their requested documents or services. This can help residents save time, reduce unnecessary visits to the barangay office, and make it easier for them to follow up on their requests.

Barangay Officials/Staff serve as the administrators of the system. They can review and process submitted requests, check the information provided by residents, update the status of requests, set claim dates, and maintain records of submitted and completed requests. The system can help officials keep requests more organized, make records easier to manage, and monitor the services that are frequently requested by residents.

Overall, the completed application benefits both residents and barangay officials by providing a more organized way to handle selected barangay service requests. It helps residents access services and receive updates more conveniently, while it helps barangay officials manage requests and records in a more systematic manner.

# 1.7 Definition of Terms

The following terms are defined based on how they are used in the proposed system:

1. **Barangay Official/Staff** – An authorized person who manages barangay services and is responsible for reviewing and processing residents' requests through the system.
2. **Claim Date** – The scheduled date set by the barangay official when a resident can claim their requested document or receive the requested service.
3. **Email Notification** – An electronic message sent to a resident's registered email address to provide updates about their service request.
4. **Online Request** – A service request submitted by a resident through the web-based system without initially needing to visit the barangay office.
5. **Request History** – A record of the previous and completed service requests submitted by a resident through the system.
6. **Request Management** – The process of reviewing, processing, updating, and managing service requests submitted by residents.
7. **Request Status** – The current condition or progress of a submitted request, such as Pending, Processing, Approved, or Completed.
8. **Resident** – A person living within Barangay Cuambugan who can use the system to access selected barangay services and submit service requests.
9. **Records Management** – The process of organizing, storing, and maintaining information about submitted and completed service requests.
10. **Service Request** – A request made by a resident to obtain a selected barangay document or service.
11. **Service List** – A section of the system that displays the selected barangay services available to residents and their requirements.
12. **System Administrator** – An authorized barangay staff member who has access to administrative features for managing users, requests, services, and records.
13. **Web-Based System** – A software application that can be accessed through a web browser using an internet connection.
14. **Barangay Cuambugan** – The selected barangay where the proposed system will be used to manage selected resident service requests.

# 2.5 System Architecture

The eBarangay Service Request System uses a web-based architecture with four main components: the web browser, application server, database, and email notification service. The web browser allows residents to view available barangay services and their requirements, submit service requests, track request statuses, and check scheduled claim dates. Barangay staff use the browser to review submitted requests, update request statuses, set claim dates, and manage service request records.

The application server processes the actions made by residents and barangay staff. It handles service requests, updates request statuses, sets claim dates, and communicates with the database to save and retrieve information. The database stores account details, available services, submitted requests, request statuses, and scheduled claim dates. This allows the system to keep information organized and make it available when needed. Meanwhile, the email notification service sends residents updates about their requests when the feature is supported and properly configured.

When residents or barangay staff access the system through a web browser, their actions are sent to the application server for processing. The server communicates with the database to save or retrieve the necessary information and then displays the results in the browser. When email notifications are enabled, residents can also receive updates about their service requests through email.

# 2.7 Database Design / ERD

The Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD) shows the structure of the database used by the eBarangay Service Request System. It consists of three main entities: Users, Services, and Requests.

The Users table stores account information, including the user's name, email, password, and role. The Services table contains information about the barangay services available in the system. The Requests table stores the service requests submitted by residents, including their descriptions, statuses, claim dates, and the staff member who processed each request.

The database has a one-to-many relationship between Users and Requests because one user can submit multiple service requests. Services and Requests also have a one-to-many relationship because one service can be requested many times. The `processed_by` field connects a request to the user who processed it, which is optional.

# Entity Relationships

**Users and Requests (One-to-Many):** One user can submit multiple service requests, but each request belongs to one user.
**Services and Requests (One-to-Many):** One service can have many submitted requests, but each request is connected to one service.
**Users and Requests through `processed_by` (One-to-Many):** One user, acting as an administrator or staff member, can process multiple requests. However, the `processed_by` field may be empty if a request has not yet been processed.

This database structure helps organize user accounts, barangay services, and service requests while keeping their related information connected.
